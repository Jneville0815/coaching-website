How I write

I do not use AI to help at all with the writing on my website (this probably goes without saying lol). For years I have been trying to start writing. Just like blog posts and stuff like that about what I'm learning. I have experimented with it a little bit, but I always have a hard time finishing something. I get in my head and overanalyze everything to the point where it just drives me crazy. Trying to get it right and trying to structure it was exhausting for me. So the way I write now is I just let it free flow. I don't think too much and just try to keep moving. I may do a second or third pass and add or update things or break things into paragraphs, but I'm also doing this pretty quickly and without getting too much in my head about it. And it's fun this way. It's fun letting it be a little messy and grammatically not the best. It feels authentic and that feels good. I don't need some fancy intro or to wrap it up in a special way. I know when it feels complete and I just go with it. This will probably change over time and that will be fun to watch happen. There's a lightness to it that feels good. Like anything goes. Okay I'm done (fancy wrap up) :)
